Frequently Asked Questions

Does LoyaltyPro work with my existing POS?

Absolutely! LoyaltyPro is hardware-agnostic and can integrate with most popular POS systems via API or simple barcode/NFC scanning.

Can I migrate my current loyalty program?

Yes. Import your existing customer data and point balances with our intuitive CSV importer or via direct API.

Do my customers need to download an app?

No mandatory apps required. Customers can use physical cards, digital wallets, or their email address to access rewards.
